About FEUARTS

Get to know us!

A community for student creatives.

Who we are

Feuture Arts is a student organization at FEU Alabang focused on multimedia arts. We bring student creatives together to share ideas, showcase their work, and grow through art.

Become part of the community →

Vision

Feuture Arts is a student organization spearheading artistry in FEU Alabang focusing on multimedia arts that promotes true artistry and efficiency for the betterment of the nation.

Mission

Feuture Arts aims to be an organization of and for the students of FEU Alabang, dedicated to the following objectives:

  • Establish a group where student artists in different fields unite in self-expression through art;
  • Showcase, develop, and improve the artistry of FEU Alabang students;
  • Leading activities that will not only be beneficial, but also bringing patriotism amongst the youth, and;
  • Enlighten society on the importance of the arts as a whole.
